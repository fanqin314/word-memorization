2 意象卡 · 一句话记住

核心画面

controversy = 两方观点迎头相撞，火花四溅。

红方 vs 蓝方 → 各持己见 → 争论不休；媒体争相报道 → public controversy；政策引发讨论 → cause controversy。
核心：对立 + 激烈 = 争议。

4 真题例句 · 语境记忆

2023 托福 · 听力

The decision to close the library sparked a great controversy among local residents.

关闭图书馆的决定在当地居民中引发了巨大争议。

2022 雅思 · 阅读

The controversy surrounding the new immigration policy has lasted for months.

围绕新移民政策的争议已经持续数月。

2021 六级 · 阅读

There is still controversy over whether genetically modified food is safe.

关于转基因食品是否安全，仍存在争议。

2020 四级 · 完形

The scientist's theory caused controversy in the academic community.

这位科学家的理论在学术界引起了争议。

5 易错提醒 · 先防错

拼写 注意 controversy：c-o-n-t-r-o-v-e-r-s-y
contraversy / controversey → controversy
搭配 "关于……的争议" 用 controversy over / about，不用 of
controversy of the law → controversy over the law
重音 英式重音在第二音节（con-TRO-ver-sy）；美式重音在首音节（CON-tro-ver-sy）

6 易混辨析 · 一句话区分

controversy争议（公众长期激烈讨论）公众激辩 controversy
dispute争端（具体纠纷/法律）具体纠纷是 dispute
debate辩论（有组织的正反方）正式辩论是 debate
argument争吵/论点（个人/理由）个人争吵 argument
quarrel吵架（言语冲突）嘴仗吵架是 quarrel
strife冲突（敌对，正式）敌对冲突用 strife

7 首字母填空 · 提取练习

先自己想完整单词，再点空格看答案。想不出来的，在纸上写一遍。

The new policy sparked a huge controversy among citizens.

提示：spark a controversy，引起争议

There is ongoing controversy over the use of AI in classrooms.

提示：controversy over，关于……的争议

The controversy surrounding the election lasted for weeks.

提示：controversy surrounding，围绕……的争议

The theory remains highly controversial among scientists.

⚠️ 陷阱：这里要形容词 controversial（有争议的）

The company was embroiled in a bitter controversy.

提示：be embroiled in controversy，卷入争议
