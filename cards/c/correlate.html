2意象卡 · 一句话记住

核心画面

correlate = 两根温度计的水银柱总是一起上升、一起下降，却谁也不决定谁。

cor-（共同）+ relate（联系）→ 一起变化、彼此相关；但相关不等于因果。

5易错提醒 · 先防错

搭配 表示“与…相关”用 correlate with，不用 correlate to。
Height correlates to weight. → Height correlates with weight.
词性 动词是 correlate，名词是 correlation，没有 correlateness 这种说法。
There is a strong correlateness. → There is a strong correlation.
逻辑 correlation 只说明相关，不能直接说成因果，写作里别写成 cause。
The correlation proves that X causes Y. → The correlation suggests a link between X and Y.

6易混辨析 · 一句话区分

correlate两者同步变化，统计上的相关income correlates with education
relate把两事联系起来；也可“讲述”relate the two events
correspond一一对应、相符；通信the numbers correspond
connect物理或逻辑上连接connect cause and effect
associate把…与…联想到一起associate red with danger
link建立联系，语气中性link diet to health

口诀：correlate 看数据，correspond 看对不对得上，associate 看脑子里怎么联想。
